About The Game

Undertale: Final Judgment is a community-driven, fan-made multiplayer battle arena game. Team up or face off against friends in intense matches supporting up to 5 players. Explore a fully interactive 3D lobby, choose from a variety of unique Sans AUs, and experience custom battle mechanics designed to capture the spirit of the original game while offering fresh, competitive gameplay.

Key Features

👥 Up to 5 Players

Engage in chaotic and strategic multiplayer battles with support for up to 5 players in a single match.

💀 Unique Sans AUs

Play as your favorite Alternate Universe versions of Sans, each with distinct abilities, attacks, and playstyles.

🌐 3D Interactive Lobby

Hang out, practice, and chat with other players in a fully realized 3D lobby environment before matches begin.

⚔️ Custom Battle Mechanics

Experience refined combat systems inspired by Undertale, adapted for fair and exciting multiplayer arena combat.
